Invoice → Bill

Read vendor invoice PDFs and create Bills in QuickBooks Online.

Milestone 1 skeleton is running. The full flow will be:

  1. Upload a vendor invoice PDF
  2. Extract the text (pdfplumber)
  3. Claude parses vendor, dates, and line items into structured JSON
  4. You review and edit the data on a form
  5. Confirm → a Bill is created in QuickBooks Online
